# TickMint Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea
TickMint turns every market tick into a clearer trading habit. The brand should feel modern, data-driven, energetic and trustworthy without looking like a broker or a speculative “get rich quick” product.

## Logo
- Primary logo: waveform/tick icon plus the `TickMint` wordmark.
- The waveform represents market movement, data and continuous improvement.
- Use `/public/tickmint-logo.svg` for horizontal placements.
- Use `/public/tickmint-icon.svg` for app icons, favicons and compact navigation.
- Preserve clear space equal to the height of one waveform bar around the logo.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour individual bars or place it over visually noisy imagery.

## Colour behaviour
- Mint/green is reserved for profit, progress, completion and positive habits.
- Red is reserved for losses, broken rules and destructive actions.
- Indigo and purple identify the TickMint product experience and should be used for CTAs, navigation and neutral analytics.
- Avoid colouring all metrics green or red. Neutral information should remain navy, slate or gray.

## Typography
- Primary family: Inter.
- Headings: 700–800 weight, compact tracking.
- Body: 400–500 weight with comfortable line-height.
- Data: 600–800 weight, tabular numbers where available.

## Voice
- Clear, calm and encouraging.
- Focus on discipline, insight and progress—not guaranteed profit.
- Preferred phrases: “Track every tick”, “See your edge”, “Finish the day with clarity”.
- Avoid hype such as “Win every trade”, “Guaranteed profit” or “Beat the market”.

## UI rules
- Light app backgrounds with white cards and restrained shadows.
- Dark navy navigation may be used for high-contrast product identity.
- Use rounded 14–22 px surfaces and generous whitespace.
- Charts should use the brand palette, but profit/loss semantics always take priority.
